FAQs

Everything you need to know about shopping with us.

We release small curated collections of preloved kids clothing called drops. Each drop has a theme and a limited number of items — once they're gone, they're gone. New drops are announced on Instagram first, so follow us at @eatyourveggies_please to be the first to know.
Browse the current drop on our site and click "Buy this" on the item you'd like. You'll be taken to a secure Stripe checkout where you can pay by card. Each item is sold individually — first in, first served.
Every item is graded before it goes into a drop. You'll see one of four condition notes on each listing: Unworn — tags on (never worn, original tags still attached), As good as new (minimal or no signs of wear), Played in gently (light wear, no damage), or Played in happily (well loved, any notable marks or wear are described). We never list anything we wouldn't be happy to put on our own kids.
Yes — we ship Australia-wide. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout. We're based in Melbourne and aim to post within 2–3 business days of your order. Local pickup in Melbourne may also be available — get in touch via Instagram if you'd like to arrange it.
It varies by drop — we stock everything from newborn up to about 14–16Y depending on what we've curated. Each item has its size listed, and you can filter by age range on the shop page to find what fits your little one.
Because all items are preloved, we're unable to offer returns or exchanges unless an item is significantly not as described. We do our best to accurately describe and photograph every piece — if something arrives and you have a concern, please reach out to us on Instagram and we'll do our best to sort it out.
We accept all major credit and debit cards via Stripe, a secure payment platform. We don't store your card details. You'll receive an order confirmation by email after checkout.
Kids grow fast — often faster than they can wear things out. Buying preloved keeps great clothes in circulation, reduces waste, and means you can dress your kids in quality brands at a fraction of the original price. It's better for the planet and easier on the wallet. Win win.
There's no set schedule — we drop when we have a collection worth dropping. Follow us on Instagram to get notified as soon as a new drop goes live.
Sold drops are archived on the site so you can browse past collections, but once an item is sold it's gone for good. Keep an eye on our Instagram for when new drops land.
We love hearing from people who want to pass on great clothes! Slide into our DMs on Instagram at @eatyourveggies_please and we'll have a chat.
